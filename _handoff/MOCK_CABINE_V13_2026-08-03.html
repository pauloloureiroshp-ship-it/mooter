🎓 Learning — the Pastor 🎓 Pastor v2 · n/d decisions · TF-IDF (Occam-aligned)
For non-devs, in 2 lines: Mooter learns from your routing decisions using classic text-comparison maths (TF-IDF) — not a neural network. Every decision is explainable and reproducible: you can ask "why this route?" and get the arithmetic, not a black-box guess.
And that is not a limitation — it is the right choice, with research behind it: arXiv 2505.12601, "Simple kNN Beats Complex Learned Routers" — simple beats complex, and we can audit simple.
"The mechanism is TF-IDF, NOT a neural LoRA. The file is named pastor-lora-status.js for continuity with the 'LoRA' routing story, but the DISPLAYED text says 'TF-IDF' so we never imply neural training." — tools/router/pastor-lora-status.js, verbatim
Honesty rules of the chip: N = real lines of decisions.log; silent below 50 decisions (DEFAULT_ON_THRESHOLD) and silent if the log is missing — honest degrade.
